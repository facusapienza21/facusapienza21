## Hello, I'm Facundo (Facu) Sapienza 👋!

I am currently a Chair of the [Multidisciplinary Institute in Artificial Intelligence](https://miai-cluster.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/miai-cluster/home-miai-1579890.kjsp?RH=6071747120751415) at the [Institute of Environmental Geosciences](https://www.ige-grenoble.fr/?lang=en) (IGE), Université Grenoble Alpes (France). Previously I completed my PhD in the Department of Statistics at the University of California Berkeley working under the supervision of Fernando Pérez and Jonathan Taylor. I finished my Bsc/Msc (Licenciatura) in Physics and Mathematics in the University of Buenos Aires 🇦🇷.

    - [Datos, Ecuaciones Diferenciales e Inteligencia Artificial](https://facusapienza.org/DM2026-Curso/): New course at University of Buenos Aires about physics-informed ML (in Spanish).
    - [Collaborative and Reproducible Data Science at UC Berkeley](https://ucb-stat-159-s23.github.io/site/): Course given to undergraduate and graduate students.
    - [Machine Learning in Glaciology Workshop](https://machine-learning-in-glaciology-workshop.github.io): Given in Finse, Norway, to students and researchers working in the intersection of cryospheric sciences 🧊 and machine learning 🤖.
